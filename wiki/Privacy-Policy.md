# Privacy policy

Project Constellation keeps its catalog locally by default and sends data only to the Google Drive or GitHub destination the user explicitly connects and configures. It does not sell conversation data, operate an advertising backend, or collect AI-provider passwords.

The complete, version-controlled policy covers processed data, storage, OAuth tokens, permissions, retention, deletion, remote sync, and contact information:

[Read the Project Constellation privacy policy](https://github.com/Herbertofury/Project-Constellation/blob/main/PRIVACY.md)

For implementation boundaries and review evidence, also see [Security and privacy](Security-and-Privacy) and [OAuth and provider setup](OAuth-and-Provider-Setup).
